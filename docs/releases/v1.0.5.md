## Kaarnegar 1.0.5

This release updates PDF reports.

### What's new

- Time entry details now show each entry's duration and amount, with total time and amounts at the bottom of the table. Amounts in different currencies are listed separately.
- The activities summary card and report footer notes have been removed from PDFs.

### Upgrading

Quit the previous version from the system tray before installing or opening this release. Your existing work history and preferences remain on your computer. Export a backup from Settings if you want a separate copy of your data.

### Downloads

- **Installer:** `Kaarnegar-Setup-1.0.5.exe`
- **Portable:** `Kaarnegar-1.0.5.exe`
- **Checksums:** `SHA256SUMS.txt`

Windows x64. No Node.js, separate font installation, account, or internet connection is required to use the app. For portable startup, keep the executable in a stable location.
